\# DecodeLabs Data Analytics - Project 4



\## Data Visualization



This project is part of my Data Analytics Internship at DecodeLabs.



The objective of this project is to transform data into clear and meaningful visualizations that communicate important patterns, trends, comparisons, and relationships.



\## Project Objective



The main objectives of this project are:



\- Create appropriate visualizations for different types of data

\- Compare categories using bar charts

\- Analyze trends over time using line charts

\- Visualize relationships using scatter plots

\- Present insights clearly through data storytelling

\- Use appropriate titles, labels, and formatting

\- Avoid unnecessary chart clutter



\## Dataset



The project uses the cleaned e-commerce dataset from the previous projects.



\- Records: 1,200

\- Columns: 14

\- Dataset: E-commerce order data



\### Main Columns



\- OrderID

\- Date

\- CustomerID

\- Product

\- Quantity

\- UnitPrice

\- ShippingAddress

\- PaymentMethod

\- OrderStatus

\- TrackingNumber

\- ItemsInCart

\- CouponCode

\- ReferralSource

\- TotalPrice

\## Visualizations Created



\### 1. Orders by Product



\*\*Chart:\*\* Horizontal Bar Chart



This visualization compares the number of orders for each product.



Key observation:



\- Printer has the highest number of orders with 181.

\- Phone has the lowest number of orders with 156.

\- The order counts are relatively close across products.



File:



`output/01\_orders\_by\_product.png`



\---



\### 2. Revenue by Product



\*\*Chart:\*\* Horizontal Bar Chart



This visualization compares the total revenue generated by each product.



Key observation:



\- Chair generates the highest revenue at approximately ₹195,620.

\- Printer follows closely at approximately ₹195,613.

\- Phone has the lowest revenue at approximately ₹151,722.



File:



`output/02\_revenue\_by\_product.png`



\---



\### 3. Monthly Order Trend



\*\*Chart:\*\* Line Chart



This visualization shows how order volume changes over time.



Key observation:



\- Monthly order volume varies across the dataset period.

\- June 2024 recorded the highest monthly order count with 53 orders.



File:



`output/03\_monthly\_order\_trend.png`



\---



\### 4. Monthly Revenue Trend



\*\*Chart:\*\* Line Chart



This visualization shows the change in revenue over time.



Key observation:



\- Monthly revenue varies considerably across the period.

\- June 2024 recorded the highest monthly revenue at ₹68,068.54.



File:



`output/04\_monthly\_revenue\_trend.png`



\---



\### 5. Payment Method Distribution



\*\*Chart:\*\* Pie Chart



This visualization shows the distribution of orders across different payment methods.



Key observation:



\- Online is the most frequently used payment method with 258 orders.

\- Gift Card has the lowest count with 230 orders.

\- The payment methods are relatively balanced.



File:



`output/05\_payment\_method\_distribution.png`



\---



\### 6. Order Status Distribution



\*\*Chart:\*\* Horizontal Bar Chart



This visualization compares the number of orders across different order statuses.



Key observation:



\- Cancelled is the most common status with 250 orders.

\- Delivered has 231 orders.

\- Cancelled and Returned together account for a substantial portion of the order statuses.



File:



`output/06\_order\_status\_distribution.png`



\---



\### 7. Quantity vs Total Price



\*\*Chart:\*\* Scatter Plot



This visualization shows the relationship between order quantity and total price.



Key observation:



\- Higher quantities are generally associated with higher order values.

\- The relationship can be explored visually through the distribution of points.



File:



`output/07\_quantity\_vs\_total\_price.png`



\## Key Insights



The visual analysis provides several useful observations:



1\. Printer has the highest order count.

2\. Chair generates the highest total revenue.

3\. Monthly order volume changes considerably over time.

4\. June 2024 recorded the highest monthly order count.

5\. June 2024 also recorded the highest monthly revenue.

6\. Online is the most frequently used payment method.

7\. Cancelled is the most common order status.

8\. Product order counts are relatively balanced.

9\. Higher quantities are generally associated with higher total order values.



\## Data Storytelling Approach



The visualizations were designed according to the type of analytical question:



\- Bar charts were used for category comparisons.

\- Line charts were used for time-based trends.

\- A scatter plot was used to examine relationships between numerical variables.

\- Titles and labels were added to make the main observations easier to understand.
